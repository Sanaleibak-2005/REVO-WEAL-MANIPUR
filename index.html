WHY REVO WEAL MANIPUR?

WHY REVO WEAL MANIPUR?

Manipur has the people, talent, resources and potential to build a stronger future. But potential becomes prosperity only when it is developed through unity, knowledge, self-reliance and collective effort.

REVO WEAL MANIPUR exists to turn that potential into real opportunity and lasting prosperity—so that people can learn, work, create, build and live with dignity.

Manipur should not merely have a future.

Manipur should have the strength to shape its own future.

CHANGE.

Revolutionaries — people committed to positive, peaceful and transformative change.

OUR VISION

OUR VISION

A Manipur where people have the knowledge to grow, the opportunity to work, the ability to create, the capacity to become self-reliant, and the dignity to build meaningful lives.

A Manipur that advances with modern development while preserving its identity, heritage and natural environment.

YOUTH & DEMOCRATIC LEADERSHIP

YOUTH & DEMOCRATIC LEADERSHIP

Youth are not merely the future of Manipur; they are part of its present responsibility.

Young people should be educated, politically aware and actively engaged in democracy—examining leaders by their character, public record, ideas, integrity and commitment to public service.

The strength of democratic change begins with informed citizens who choose their representatives thoughtfully and hold them accountable.

Do not follow blindly. Understand. Question. Participate. Choose responsibly.

THE INSPIRATION OF HIJAM IRABOT

THE INSPIRATION OF HIJAM IRABOT

REVO WEAL MANIPUR draws inspiration from Hijam Irabot, an important figure in modern Manipur's history whose public life was associated with social reform, education, political awakening, peasant organization, cultural development and public participation.

His legacy reminds us that lasting change requires an aware, educated and responsible people.

We carry forward the values of:

Unity.
Education.
Social uplift.
Dignity of working people.
People's participation.
Cultural strength.
Self-reliance.
Collective responsibility.

We respect the lessons of the past while developing our own response to the challenges of the present.
